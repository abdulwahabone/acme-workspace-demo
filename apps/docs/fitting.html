Getting set up

NEW · Fitting and first use

Three of the four filters fit by hand in under fifteen minutes. The fourth goes on the rising main and wants a plumber.

Before you start

  • Run the cold tap for thirty seconds so the standing water clears.
  • Take the cartridge out of its card sleeve; keep the sleeve for the date.
  • Wash your hands, then handle the cartridge by its ends.

Ripple Pitcher and Carafe Mini

  1. Soak the cartridge in cold water for five minutes.
  2. Push it into the funnel until it clicks. It will not sit flush before it clicks.
  3. Fill and discard the first two jugs. The water may look grey; that is carbon dust.
  4. Write the date on the fridge label and stick it on the jug.

Tap Clip

  1. Unscrew the aerator from the end of the spout and keep it somewhere safe.
  2. Pick the rubber collar that fits your spout: the thick one for 16 to 20 mm, the thin one for 20 to 24 mm.
  3. Hand tighten the housing. No spanner, or the thread will strip.
  4. Run filtered water for one minute before you drink it.

Do not fit to a boiling tap

The Tap Clip is rated to 38°C. On an instant hot tap the housing will deform, and a deformed housing leaks.

Undersink Duo

  1. Turn off the cold feed under the sink and open the tap to drop the pressure.
  2. Screw the bracket to the cupboard wall, not to the floor of the cupboard.
  3. Push the hoses fully home; the fitting grips about 18 mm of pipe.
  4. Open the feed, check every joint for two minutes, then flush ten litres through the drinking tap.
cold feed --> stage 1 sediment --> stage 2 carbon --> drinking tap

Whole Home Guard

This one cuts into the rising main and needs isolation valves either side. If the words "isolation valve" are new, book a plumber; an hour of labour costs less than a flooded hallway.

When to change the cartridge

Change on the date, not on the taste. Carbon carries on removing chlorine long after it stops removing anything else, so water that still tastes fine can be running through a spent cartridge.
