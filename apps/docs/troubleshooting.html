If it goes wrong

When something looks wrong

Most of what gets reported in the first week is normal, and the rest is usually a cartridge that has not seated. Here is how to tell them apart.

Black specks in the first jug

Carbon dust. It is harmless and it is why the fitting page says to throw the first two jugs away. If specks are still coming through after five litres, the cartridge has split and should be replaced.

The flow has slowed to a trickle

Sediment has blocked the first stage. On a jug that means the cartridge is at the end of its life; on an Undersink Duo it usually means the 5 micron stage, not the carbon one. 

slow, and water tastes fine   --> sediment stage
slow, and water tastes of chlorine --> both stages

The Tap Clip drips where it meets the spout

  1. Turn the tap off and unscrew the housing by hand.
  2. Check the rubber collar is the right one; a 16 mm collar on a 22 mm spout will always weep.
  3. Refit hand tight. If it still drips, the spout thread is worn, and no clamp will fix that.

Water where it should not be

A puddle in the cupboard under the sink is not a filter problem to work through page by page. Turn the cold feed off first, then look.

The cartridge will not click in

The Ripple Pitcher and the Carafe Mini take different cartridges. The Mini is 20 mm shorter and will drop straight through a Ripple funnel; the full size Cascade will not seat in a Carafe at all. Check the sleeve before you push harder.

Limescale came back after a month

A jug or a tap filter treats the water you drink, not the water the boiler heats. If the kettle is scaling, the filter has to be on the rising main. See Choosing a filter for which one that is.
